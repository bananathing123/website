REEF RUN

Roll, dive, grab pearls, haul them home.

📖 How to play

  • 🎯 Goal: be first to bank 5 points. A pearl is worth 1, the golden chest is worth 2. Pearls are scattered at random each game, always mirrored so it stays fair. It surfaces in the middle once each player has had two turns.
  • 🎲 Your turn: roll two dice. Use each die to move one of your three divers (any diver, any order, up to that many steps, no diagonals). You may use both dice on the same diver.
  • 🎒 Carry: end a move on a pearl or the chest to pick it up. A diver carries one thing at a time. Swim back to your home row to bank it. The chest is heavy: whenever you move its carrier, each die counts for half (rounded up). A 6 moves him 3, a 5 moves him 3, a 4 moves him 2.
  • 💥 Bump: end a move on an enemy diver to send them back to their home row. They drop whatever they were carrying right there, and you can scoop it up. Divers on their own home row are safe.
  • 🪸 Terrain: rocks block the way, kelp costs 2 steps to enter, and ending on a whirlpool sweeps you to the other whirlpool.
  • ⚖️ Tiebreaker: if every piece is banked and the score is tied 4-4, a fresh golden chest appears in the middle. Whoever banks it wins.
  • 🎲🎲 Doubles: roll the same number twice and, after using both dice, you choose: swap one of your divers with any opponent diver (swapping ends your turn; divers on their home row are safe from swaps), or roll again. You can only roll again once per turn; if you roll doubles a second time, you can swap or end your turn.
  • 🌊 Tide token: once per game, after rolling and before moving, you may spend your token to reroll both dice. You must keep the new result.
  • 🕳️ Hidden Mode (Settings): a much bigger reef (23×23). Each player has 8 divers, you roll 6 dice and can use each one, the reef holds 16 pearls and 2 golden chests (they surface together), and each player has 2 tide tokens. First to 10 points wins. Bonus rule: instead of doubles, rolling three or more of a kind lets you swap or roll again.
  • 🪙 Who starts: chosen at random.
  • 🤖 Bot levels: Easy wanders and misses chances. Medium plays a solid game. Hard races you for the golden chest, hunts your carriers and plans both dice together.
