How to Play

Player 1: A/D move · W toss / jump · SPACE spike
Player 2: ←/→ move · ↑ toss / jump · RIGHT SHIFT spike
(In 1 Player mode both key sets work for you.)

Spiking: jump and hold your spike key as the ball arrives, on any of your 3 touches. Time freezes and the arrow sweeps; release the key to hit. Aim at the center of the net for max power.

Jammed: if someone smashes a power shot and you receive it up close to the net, your pass goes wild (or your counter-spike is weak). Stay back.

Rally: 3 touches per team, then the ball must hit the net. If the ball bounces on the net twice in a row, the team that hit it in loses the point. Hit the ground and the team that failed loses the point.

Modes: 1 Player, 2P Co-op (both of you vs CPU), 1v1, or 2v2 with a CPU partner each. In 1v1 modes there are no partners, so you get only 2 hits each rally: one to set up, one to spike. 1v1 CPU is you vs a single CPU.
